# How AI Works

Pattern Recognition at Scale

Modern AI works by finding patterns in enormous amounts of data, then using those patterns to generate predictions or responses — without being explicitly programmed for every scenario.

Analyzes vast datasets to recognize trends and correlations

Uses algorithms and computational power to interpret complex situations

Makes predictions and decisions autonomously once trained

AI doesn't follow rigid rules — it learns from examples and generalizes to new situations.

# Types of AI

Narrow AI

Designed for specific tasks — image recognition, language translation, game playing. Most AI systems today fall into this category, excelling within a single domain.

General-Purpose AI

Can handle many different types of tasks across domains. Large language models are emerging as increasingly versatile general-purpose tools.

Understanding Large Language Models

A large language model (LLM) is a type of AI program that can recognize and generate text — trained on huge sets of data, hence the word large. LLMs are built on machine learning, specifically a neural network architecture called transformers.

# The Transformer Architecture

The Engine Behind Every LLM

The transformer is a set of neural networks consisting of an encoder and a decoder with self-attention capabilities — extracting meaning from sequences of text and understanding relationships between words and phrases.

Processes entire sequences in parallel rather than word-by-word

Leverages GPUs for training, dramatically reducing training time

Handles very large datasets and complex language patterns at scale
