# Agentic AI

AI that acts on its own.

# What is Agentic AI?

Plan, decide, act

AI that handles multi-step tasks on its own.

Memory + context

It remembers, learns, and adapts.

Acts first

It takes initiative, not just prompts.

# Agentic AI vs. Generative AI: Key Differences

What Sets Them Apart

Generative AI: creates content from prompts.

Agentic AI: plans and takes action.

Both: use LLMs, tools, and context.

# Core Capabilities of Agentic AI

Autonomous Planning

Breaks big goals into steps.

Tool Integration

Uses APIs and systems.

Memory & Context

Remembers what happened before.

Decision-Making

Adjusts when things change.
